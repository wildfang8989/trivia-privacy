Privacy Policy for TrIVia

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Willow IV Studios publishes TrIVia, an offline single-player trivia game for Android. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information collected by the app

TrIVia does not require account registration and, in its current version, does not request personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or location. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to Willow IV Studios.

Information stored on your device

TrIVia saves game-related information, including your personal high scores, locally on your device so you can view your progress. These scores are not uploaded to Willow IV Studios or shared with other players. TrIVia's trivia content and gameplay are available offline.

Data sharing and third-party services

The current version of TrIVia does not include advertising, analytics, account services, in-app purchases, online multiplayer, or social sharing. Willow IV Studios does not sell or share personal data collected through the app because the app does not collect such data.

Google Play and your Android device may independently process information when you download, install, or use apps, under their own terms and privacy policies. That processing is separate from TrIVia's in-app data handling.

Permissions and security

TrIVia is designed to function without network access or access to sensitive device permissions. Locally stored game information is kept in the app's private storage area and is managed by the Android operating system.

Retention and deletion

High scores remain on your device until you clear TrIVia's app storage or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. You can remove locally stored game data through Android Settings > Apps > TrIVia > Storage > Clear storage (wording varies by device). Willow IV Studios has no remotely stored player records to delete.

Children's privacy

TrIVia does not require children or adults to provide personal information, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game's age rating may vary by country or region.

Changes to this policy

If TrIVia's features or information-handling practices change, Willow IV Studios will update this policy and its effective date. Any new online functionality, analytics, or other data collection will be described in an updated policy.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or TrIVia's data handling, contact willowivstudios@gmail.com.
